# Tradeblock

> Tradeblock (Astrolab, Inc. d/b/a Tradeblock, founded 2019) is a mobile-first, peer-to-peer sneaker trading marketplace where collectors use their sneakers as currency. Members build a Closet and Wishlist, browse public and private trade offers, and swap sneakers with other collectors, balancing uneven trades with cash. Every trade is verified through Tradeblock's authentication centers before shipping. Tradeblock is a consumer app (iOS, Android, web) and does not publish a public developer API, SDKs, or a developer portal.
